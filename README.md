LendRE Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 21, 2026

LendRE is a set of mortgage and real estate calculators. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

Information we collect
We do not collect, store or share any personal information. LendRE has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information you enter
The numbers you type into the calculators, and any projects you save, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing
When you choose to share a PDF or copy a summary, it goes only where you send it, using your device's standard share options.

Internet connections
The app downloads display fonts and a PDF-generation library from public content delivery networks (Google Fonts and Cloudflare). These requests do not include any information you enter.

Children
LendRE is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
